I’m a business-management consultant and software engineer. I build software that runs the business, not just software the business runs. I spent over a decade in enterprise engineering. That work ran from defense-grade systems to public-sector microservices. It taught me where real operations break. Now I design self-hosted platforms and AI agents that close those gaps. They live inside the client’s own stack. I focus on real estate and property management.

At NLMA I built and self-host 7+ MCP servers and 40+ live services. They open up 20+ SaaS and data platforms as OAuth 2.1-secured tools. LLM agents call those tools directly. Autonomous multi-agent systems run on top of them. Those agents handle triage, reconciliation, and code fixes end to end. Human-in-the-loop guardrails keep them in check.

Most AI never makes it into production. My edge is the opposite. I’m a forward-deployed engineer who ships working, self-hosted AI systems. Clients own all of it: the code, the infrastructure, and the outcomes.
