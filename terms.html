Discovery consultations, authorization holds, and transcript review

When you book a free discovery consultation, we place an authorization hold of $112.50 — a 15-minute increment of advisory time at our standard $450/hour rate — on the card you provide. A hold reserves funds; it is not a charge. The hold is released, and no charge is made, unless the call becomes paid advisory work, and nothing is ever captured without a person at NLMA reviewing and approving it.

To keep the hold valid through the date of your call, we store your card as a payment method on file and may re-authorize it shortly before the consultation. If a re-authorization does not succeed, we will ask you to reconfirm your card; a consultation we are unable to secure may be cancelled.
